08 / Risks and limitations

Board performance

TCG and ISA-specific crypto affect CPU and TCP rates. Verify PHY, clocks, entropy, thermal behavior and real throughput.

USB and Ethernet

Prove simultaneous gadget enumeration, host reconnect and Ethernet uplink. Use a board-specific kernel and device tree.

Storage and reliability

Allow for cache growth, ECC and NAND wear. Exercise power loss and sustained traffic. Public Tor timings vary between circuits.
